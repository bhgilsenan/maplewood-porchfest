Typography

Their whole site — headings and body both — runs on Kepler Std, a licensed Adobe/Typekit serif we can't load in a page they'll paste as raw embed code. The structure below borrows their instinct (one warm editorial serif carrying the content) while swapping in free faces, and keeps a plain sans for the small interactive chrome where a serif would hurt legibility.
